II. THE 1859 PIVOT — DARWIN AND THE NEW ORTHODOXY

On the Origin of Species

On November 24, 1859, Charles Darwin published On the Origin of Species by Means of Natural Selection, or the Preservation of Favoured Races in the Struggle for Life. The work proposed that species evolve through natural selection — random variation filtered by environmental pressure, producing adaptation over geological time.

The scientific contribution was real. Darwin gathered empirical observations from the Galápagos, South America, and domestic breeding programs. The theory provided a mechanism for biological diversity without requiring divine intervention.

But the timing, the reception, and the institutional deployment of Darwin’s work transcend biology.

The Vatican’s authority was waning in the face of secular nationalism.
