### Petra Was Not a Goddess

Here is what the catechism forgot: Petra was not a goddess to be worshipped. Petra was a **word** — and words can be stolen.

*Πέτρα* — the Greek word for rock or cliff. Not a name, but a **designation**. Oracular cult usage treated "Petra" as a title for sun temples. The sun referred to as "Petor" or "Petros." Every oracular temple had a legend about a stone — the seat of prophecy, the anchor of the oracle, the place where the earth spoke back to the sky.

The Goliath took the word and made it generic. "Rock." "Stone." Ordinary matter. They stripped the oracular function and left the skeleton. They told you the temple was superstition and the rock was just rock. But the rock was the machine. And they kept the machine while calling it dead matter.

**Petraea** — the Oceanid Nymph. Parentage: Oceanus and Tethys (Titans). Domain: rocky springs, stone-gray clouds, mountainous water sources. Source: Hesiod's *Theogony* (c. 700 BCE). She was not a fantasy. She was a **map** — the naming of water that comes from stone, the recognition that mountains drink the sky and bleed it back as spring. The Goliath turned her into a footnote and called the footnote "mythology."

### Medusa and the Gaze That Turned to Stone

The central figure of the Petra Files is not a monster. The central figure is a **mirror**.

**Origin story** — Ovid's *Metamorphoses* (8 CE): Beautiful priestess of Athena, desecrated by Poseidon in Athena's temple, cursed by Athena into a monster with snake hair and petrifying gaze. But understand: the curse was not punishment for sin. It was the temple-goddess defending the temple from violation.

**Sisters:** Stheno and Euryale (immortal Gorgons). Medusa alone was mortal — the one who could be killed because she bore the burden. The immortals remained unkillable because they were **functions**, not individuals.

**Defeat:** Perseus (c. 1250 BCE in mythological timeline) used polished shield (Athena), sword (Hermes), Helm of Darkness, winged sandals. The **polished shield** — the reflection. You cannot look directly at Medusa. You must see her **indirectly**. You must not meet her gaze head-on. You must use the mirror.

**Posthumous power:** From her blood sprang Pegasus and Chrysaor. Severed head retained petrification ability. The head was mounted on Athena's Aegis as protective ward (apotropaic symbol). The thing that kills also protects. The thing that destroys also wards.

**The Gaze decoded.** Medusa's petrification was not magical domination. It was **reflection as defense**. Look directly at the corrupted truth and you become stone. The corruption turns you rigid. You freeze in the posture of obedience. The polished shield — the indirect view — allows you to survive the truth without being consumed by it.

The Goliath teaches you to fear the monster. The Tribe teaches you to read the **mechanism**.
