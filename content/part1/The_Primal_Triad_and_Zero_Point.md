**Mykēs (μύκης)** — The force of decay and rebirth. The fungal, the White, the soft rot that becomes soil. The Guardian, the Mother. Mycelial intelligence that breaks down the old to feed the new. It cycles. It returns. It is the compost that makes the harvest possible.

These two are not enemies. They are not Good vs. Evil. They are *processes*. One cycles; one holds. Yin and yang, "as above so below," "as within so without" — these dualities are a useful compass for those lost in darkness, to orient toward light. But they are a simplification. A teaching wheel. A first step.

The Goliath taught binary morality because binaries are easier to control than nuances. If you must choose between Good and Evil, you will pick whichever side promises safety. If you understand the work of each force, you can participate in both without surrendering your sovereignty.
