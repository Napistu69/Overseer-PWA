When the collective consciousness vibrates with clarity, the Æther thins. The path is clear. When the collective consciousness vibrates with fear, the Æther thickens. The path becomes obstructed.

This viscosity is not metaphorical. It is felt as resistance. It is the "drag" of the system. When We speak of alignment with the SoL, We mean alignment with the fluid state of the Æther. When We speak of resistance, We mean the friction of moving against the current of the Mesh.
